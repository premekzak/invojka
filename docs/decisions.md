# Decision log

Newest first. Each entry records what was decided and why, and who decided when it was the owner's call.

## 2026-10-11

- **The design system is colour-agnostic** (the owner's request: the scheme may change later). It names only colour roles (`accent`, `danger`, `surface`…), never hues or hex. Its tokens hold role values directly, with no palette layer, and it lists the contrast checks any new scheme must pass. The current Ledger values pass all of them. Scheme-specific exceptions (the picker-pure accent) stay in [ui.md](ui.md). The app already has this split: palette in `src/index.css`, roles everywhere else.
- **A design system, "Invoicer Ledger", with a forms standard** (the owner's request; references: GitHub, Cloudflare and Rechain). It records the current tokens and adds rules for fields: the label above in 14 px `fg`, the hint under the field, a 2 px blue focus edge, an error message with an icon, required fields marked with `*`, and the save button kept enabled so errors show when it's pressed. It is published as a private Design System artifact and isn't in the app yet. → [ui.md](ui.md)
- **Field border: the "ledger line"** (the owner's choice from five options). The top and sides are `line` (`#d4d6d8`; dark `#343538`). The bottom edge is the existing `input` role with a new, lighter value (`#86898d`; dark `#6b6e72`), which reaches 3.3:1 or more on card and paper. Black text inside a dark grey box looked heavy. A plain hairline (the GitHub look, 1.5:1) was rejected because it fails the 3:1 rule for controls; the bottom edge keeps fields easy to find. → [ui.md](ui.md)
- **The forms standard is in the app** (the owner's go-ahead). Every field now uses one `.control` box. Labels are 14 px `fg` with a hint or error under the field, required fields get `*`, and units sit inside the field. Save buttons stay enabled and show errors instead of being disabled, and app settings are rows. One shared `Select` replaced four copied class strings. Selects and text areas now get a focus edge. → [ui.md](ui.md)
- **Working rule: design changes go into the design system too** (the owner's request). When the owner asks for a design change, update the design system as well. If the request breaks one of its principles, say so first. → `CLAUDE.md`

## 2026-10-01

- **No paper colour: a very light grey page** (the owner's request, chosen over a very light blue). The page is `#f6f7f8`, cards are white, and all greys are near-neutral with a faint cool tint; the beige is gone. A neutral base keeps `#0000ff` the only hue that says "click here". A white page with the beige greys was tried briefly first. → [ui.md](ui.md)
- **One box pattern: the title goes above the box, never inside** (the owner noticed both patterns in use). The Home chart titles and settings group titles moved out of their boxes. The customer form was split into two titled boxes ("Údaje odběratele", "Výchozí hodnoty faktur"), and its right column got a "Faktury" title. Bordered boxes inside a box became grey-filled panels, and padding was unified to 20 px. → [ui.md](ui.md)
- **Five font sizes: 36, 24, 18, 14, 12 px** (the owner's request). Previously eight sizes were used. Changes: 20 px became 18 (the Home summary) or 24 (the customer stats, as big numbers); 16 px became 14 (large button text) or 18 (the logo letter); chart text went from 11 to 12 px. `body` now defaults to 14 px, which also fixed two places that silently rendered at the browser's 16 px (the sidebar company name and the "Co dál" card titles). → [ui.md](ui.md)
- **Identity colour: black instead of navy** (the owner's request). Titles, the logo and the next-step icons use the ink colour, so blue is the only accent again. Last year's chart bars went back to pale blue. → [ui.md](ui.md)
- **Theme: Ledger** (the owner's choice from five paper themes: Studio, Ledger, Manila, Newsprint, Airmail). Cream paper `#f9f4e7`, white cards and warm greys. Blue `#0000ff` is for everything clickable. Navy `#0f2a5f` is a second interface colour for identity (titles, logo, next-step icons, never clickable); this is the owner's deliberate exception to "one accent". Red covers warnings and errors, and green is for short confirmations. Amber was dropped: the owner expects warnings to be common and errors rare, so one red covers both, with warnings as pale red chips. "Paid" became plain grey text, so it stays apart from the Overdue chip in grayscale. This supersedes the earlier entries today on white plus four greys and on amber. → [ui.md](ui.md)
- **Colour roles: blue for actions, red for errors and delete, amber for warnings** (the owner's choices). Overdue moved from red to amber, because the owner keeps red for real errors and alerts. Amber was picked over orange, ochre and marker yellow. Warnings are light amber chips with ink text, so they also differ from red in lightness. Paid and cancelled badges moved to the lighter fill grey, so Overdue stays distinct in grayscale. → [ui.md](ui.md)
- **The neutrals were cut to white plus four greys** (the owner's request). The page, cards and sidebar are white, and four greys cover fill, line, secondary text and ink. This replaces the cream and kraft 12-step scale from earlier the same day. Secondary and caption text merged into one mid grey, and translucent tints became solid tones. → [ui.md](ui.md)
- **Grayscale phase: all colour switched off, to be added back one role at a time** (the owner's request). The hierarchy has to work on lightness, weight and labels alone first. The colour roles point at grey-scale steps, and the old values are kept in `src/index.css`. Text links became underlined, and the overdue badge became a solid dark chip, so neither depends on colour. → [ui.md](ui.md)
- **The neutrals became one 12-step warm scale** (the owner approved it after a grayscale comparison). Before, there were ten hand-picked greys. Secondary and caption text looked the same (1.2:1 apart), the sidebar, hover fill and border were within 2 lightness points, and field borders were 1.3:1. Now there are three distinct text levels, evenly spaced papers, and field borders at 3:1 or more (the `color` skill's rule for interactive parts). The look stays cream, white and kraft. Dark mode also got `color-scheme: dark` for native controls. → [ui.md](ui.md)
- **The plain CSS blue `#0000ff` stays** (the owner's choice). It was compared side by side with three calmer blues (ultramarine `#1d29bb`, cobalt `#0554af`, navy `#113d70`) in light, dark and grayscale. It remains a recorded exception to the "no picker-default colours" rule and is limited to small elements. → [ui.md](ui.md)
- **The invoice PDF stays neutral** (the owner's choice). It has no accent colour: warm ink greys and a near-black total box. Its colours now live in one place. → [ui.md](ui.md)
- **Colour rules now live in a user-level `color` skill**, written from the owner's chat on what makes professional colour schemes work. The rules: one accent, tinted neutrals, ramps in OKLCH, role tokens, states by rule, hierarchy by lightness, and a designed dark mode. The current palette was measured against it. It mostly passes. Pure `#0000ff` is kept as the owner's deliberate exception, limited to small elements. The gaps are listed in the roadmap and haven't been fixed yet. → [ui.md](ui.md)

## 2026-09-30

- **The Home screen's number tiles became a sentence, and the greeting became a joke by time of day** (the owner's request). The sentence is easier to read than four boxes and can say what matters ("all of it is overdue"). Greetings don't use a name, because Czech needs the vocative case. → [ui.md](ui.md)
- **Demo mode anonymizes the owner's own data in memory rather than showing generic sample data.** The owner wants screenshots and videos without creating data or touching files. Anonymizing the real data keeps the screens realistic: the same number of invoices, the same statuses and dates. At first, amounts were scaled by one hidden factor. The owner then asked for the amounts and charts to be made up too, so amounts are now invented per month and don't reveal real revenue or trends. → [architecture.md](architecture.md#demo-mode)
- **Colour scheme: beige papers + plain CSS blue, red only for overdue** (the owner's choice after six rounds of mockups). Cream content, kraft sidebar, blue headlines, buttons, links and charts; last year's bars are a pale blue tint. This replaces the petrol/teal/ochre scheme. The owner wanted fewer colours and text that stands out. → [ui.md](ui.md)
- **Bigger, bolder headlines** (the owner's request). Page titles are 36 px and section titles 24 px, all in Newsreader 600. Using one serif weight made the file about 50 kB smaller.
- **Typography review** (the owner's request, against Google Fonts, Material 3, Ramotion and Mosaiq). Fixes: embed Inter 500, because `font-medium` was rendering as regular; raise faint grey and warning text to 4.5:1; chart text at least 11 px; page titles in Newsreader 400 without negative tracking; tabular Inter instead of monospace for numbers; drop Inter `cv11`. The file grew by about 130 kB. → [ui.md](ui.md)
- **Warm paper look: cream background, serif headlines (Newsreader), Inter for everything else, small corners (max 6px), no all-caps labels, no shadows; teal and ochre charts.** The owner's choice. The serif is embedded as small woff2 subsets and used only in the UI, not in the PDF. The radius scale is capped in `@theme` so every `rounded-*` class stays subtle. → [ui.md](ui.md)
- **Exchange rates come from ECB (automatic), ČNB files (uploaded) and manual entry.** The owner allowed external requests as long as no user data is sent, and asked for manual entry. ČNB can't be fetched because of CORS; a proxy was rejected because it routes requests through a third party. → [exchange-rates.md](exchange-rates.md)
- **Revenue charts on the Home screen, by month (this year vs last) and by year.** The owner asked for them. They're hand-built SVG with no chart library, to keep the single file small and styling fully under control.
- **Foreign-currency revenue without a rate is excluded, and the UI says so.** A silent zero would under-report the NZD years.
- **Issuing an invoice saves its PDF into the data folder** (`pdf/<year>/<number>.pdf`), so every issued invoice has its document on disk.
- **PDFs use `@react-pdf/renderer` with embedded Inter TTFs.** They render the same everywhere and can be saved straight into the folder; the browser's print dialog can't do that.
- **QR Platba only for unpaid CZK invoices.** Czech banking apps expect CZK, and paying a paid invoice makes no sense.
- **A customer can be created from the invoice's customer search**, and "Edit customer" saves the invoice as a draft first. This came from the owner's feedback that the app should guide them and not lose work.
- **Drafts follow customer edits; issued invoices keep their snapshot.** An issued invoice must match what was sent.
- **Home screen with "Co dál" (Next steps) cards.** The owner found the first post-setup screen confusing ("it does nothing").
- **Onboarding Finish waits for the save; 0-byte files count as missing.** Fixes a real incident in the owner's OneDrive folder.
- **The source template is renamed to `index.html`; the only `invoicer.html` is the build output.** The owner opened the template and saw a blank page.
- **Single bundled HTML file** (the owner's choice). Tested: it works from `file://`, including folder access.
- **JSON, one file per type** (the owner's choice over one file per invoice). Sync conflicts are handled by a `lastModified` check and a conflict banner.
- **JSON rather than YAML.** YAML silently changes values: `01234567` becomes a number and loses its leading zero, and `no` becomes a boolean.
- **One business profile per data folder.** A different business uses a different folder, which keeps the model and numbering simple.
- **Chrome and Edge first, storage behind an adapter.** The File System Access API is Chromium-only; Firefox and Safari can get a browser-storage adapter with zip export later.
- **Stack:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind, Zustand, Zod, i18next, date-fns, papaparse. Common, typed, and small enough for one file.
- **Money in minor units, dates as ISO strings, IDs as strings, totals always computed.** See [data-model.md](data-model.md).
