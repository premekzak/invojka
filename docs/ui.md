# UI

## Principles (from the owner)

- **Always show the next step.** The Home screen's table of things to check (under the greeting, next to the "Nová faktura" tile; captioned "Co dál" cards until 2026-10-17) comes from the data: create an invoice, N overdue invoices, missing profile fields, review customers. New screens should do the same.
- **Smooth and fast:**
  - keyboard-first editors: the customer search works with ↑ ↓ Enter; Enter in a line description adds the next line, which takes focus
  - no page reloads
  - short rise animations (`animate-rise`)
  - hover actions on list rows
- **Never lose work:** autosave with a visible status; Finish and Issue wait for the save to finish; leaving the editor through "Upravit odběratele" (Edit customer) saves a draft first.
- **Explain in plain words.** The owner isn't a developer. Error and confirmation text says what happened and what to do.

  - **Colours from the owner:** the blue comes from `#099bc1` (also tried: `#029bca`), the orange is `#fa8d4e`. Both fail contrast on white as they are (3.3:1 and 2.3:1), so the blue is darkened to `#077491` for text and white-text fills, `#099bc1` itself is used for chart bars, and the orange appears only as small marks or behind dark text.
  - **Owner's exceptions to the colour rules, recorded so nobody "fixes" them:** the peach header band is a large tinted area (the rule says large areas stay very muted); the page is faintly cream rather than white; orange is a second hue next to the blue accent, kept to the logo, never to anything clickable.
  - **Status on the header:** green and crimson reach only 3.1:1 and 3.7:1 on peach, so the save status in the header is a tick in header text, and an error is a solid crimson chip with `danger-fg`.
  - **Rust for warnings, crimson for errors** (2026-10-17). The owner found red aggressive, so overdue and missing rates are rust, a much darker step of the orange (L 0.50 vs 0.75), always with a label. Errors and delete stay crimson, because they are rare and must stand out. The Home tile icons are ink (overdue: rust), so in dark mode the logo is the only identity orange next to the rust.
  - **One gradient: the footer** (2026-10-17). Scheme "A" tried subtle gradients on primary buttons and the header band; the owner found both too much the same day, so buttons are flat `accent` (hover `accent-deep`) and the header is flat. Later the same day the owner asked for a footer with a very slight one, so that is the only gradient now.
  - **Back to F4, brighter** (2026-10-17, later the same day): the owner asked for the colours from before "A", glowing a bit more, with very subtle gradients. F4's teal accent was tried and the owner asked for v0.2.0's azure (`#006fb5`) back, so only the header changed: "O1" `#fea668`, picked from three steps between F4's peach and A's orange. Gradients were tried on the header and the tile and found too much again, so everything is flat. A's layout stays: borderless cards on the darker page.
  - _Colours superseded by "Back to F4"; the layout stays._ **Scheme "A"** (2026-10-17): the header orange darker (`#f28d50`, flat, picked as "O3" from six steps), the blue moved from teal (hue 224) to azure (hue 245), a bluer chart. Buttons and links share `accent` `#006fb5` (white text 5.3:1).
  - **Status labels are all one style** (the owner's choice "T-C", 2026-10-17; it replaced the 2026-10-11 "green text with a check" for Paid): a soft rounded label (`rounded-[12px]`, the pill radius) with a 6 px dot and the word. Draft: `surface-2`, a hollow dot. Issued: `surface-2`, a `line-strong` dot. Overdue: `warning-chip` with rust. Paid: `success-chip` with green. Cancelled: `surface-2`, struck through. The words always say it, so grayscale and colour blindness are fine.
  - **Text links are underlined** (`underline underline-offset-2 decoration-line-strong`, darker on hover), so colour alone doesn't mark them.
  - **State rule:**
    - Fill hover is `/90`.
    - Rows, list and menu items, ghost and icon buttons hover to `tint` (2026-10-19; before, `surface-2` or `row-hover`, which read as grey).
    - Filled neutral buttons (secondary) hover one step (`surface-2` → `surface-3`).
    - Outlined hover (fields, choices) darkens the border to `line-strong`; borderless clickable cards turn `tint`.
    - Selected is `surface-2` with an accent border.
    - Focus is an accent border plus a `line` ring.
    - Disabled is `opacity-50`.
    - No translucent tints for surfaces. Only these state effects and the modal scrim (`bg-scrim/50`) are translucent.
  - **Contrast:** every text colour reaches 4.5:1 on paper, card and fill in both modes, and field borders are well above 3:1. Dark mode sets `color-scheme: dark`, so native controls match.
  - **Checked 2026-10-01:** a computed contrast sweep of every text element and field border on Home, Invoices, Customers, Settings and the invoice editor, in light and dark mode, plus a grayscale screenshot of the invoice list. Everything passes.
  - **PDF:** neutral by default, the owner's choice (warm ink greys, a near-black "to pay" box). The cancelled and paid stamps are ink too. Since 2026-10-18 a user may pick one accent in Settings → Vzhled faktury (blue, teal, green, wine, orange; each at least 4.5:1 with white text); it colours only the title and the "to pay" box, also on the cash receipt, so the PDF still prints well in black and white. ## Colour schemes

Settings → App → Barevné schéma (`settings.ui.scheme`). Light and dark come from Vzhled, so every scheme has both. The default `warm` is Warm paper, defined in `src/index.css`. Every other scheme is a file in `src/schemes/` that redefines the palette (the `--paper` … `--peach` slots, the header pill and the footer) under `<html data-scheme="…">`; components never change.

How a scheme is made: the owner puts images and a brief (`zadani.md`) in `schemes/<name>/` (only the brief is committed, images may be under copyright). The palette is built in OKLCH, every text pair is computed to 4.5:1 and edges and chart bars to 3:1 in both modes, and a preview in the real app comes before it is wired in. A dark header needs its own `--color-header-active` (the default pill is the page colour, which only works on a light header).

**The Neon family** (2026-10-22, the owner: "smaž všechny schémata kromě defaultu. A zlepšuj neon, udělej variace, ale neměň jen barvy, zajímá mě podíl jednotlivých barev, práce se stupni šedi"). Every other scheme was removed; what is left is Warm paper and five Neons that share **one neon (lime, OKLCH 0.9 / 0.21 / 128, black text on it at 4.8:1) and one black**, and differ in **how much of each there is and how the greys are built**:
- **Shares.** Neon: about 70 % white and light grey, 20 % black, 10 % neon. Grafit: greys 60 %, graphite 25 %, neon under 2 %. Výboj: neon about 30 % (header, footer, track, hover). Křída: about 90 % white, black only as type and lines.
- **Greys.** Neon: four even steps from a light page. Grafit: a darker page and a deep ramp, so the white cards stand out strongly and tables read by grey bands. Křída: no step at all between page and cards; only lines part them. Ocel: the same steps as Neon, but cool (blue-grey) instead of neutral. Neon, Grafit, Výboj and Křída have zero chroma in every grey.
- **Where the neon sits.** A light fill with black text (tile, buttons) or, inverted, black fills with neon text (Výboj, Grafit's tile). It is never text on white: links and focus are a dark green step (L ≈ 0.5, 4.6:1). In charts it is last year's bar (a pale series, exempt from 3:1); this year is black, graphite or steel. Grafit drops it from the charts altogether.
- **Status** chips stay pale (C ≤ 0.03); success moves to hue 165 and warning to 65, away from the lime.
- **Current tab on a white header** (Křída, Lilek; the owner, 2026-10-26: with no fill and no sides it looked odd): the tab is outlined left, right and top in the header line's colour (`--scheme-tab-edge`) and breaks the line, like an open folder tab.
- **Recorded exceptions:** the neon action has no 3:1 edge on white (its black label carries it, as for any light-fill action); Křída's page equals the cards (as the earlier white schemes); Křída's header line uses `--scheme-header-image`.

Every pair (63 per mode) was printed and passes. The generator lives outside the repo.

**The dark-gradient round** (2026-10-26, the owner: "nech ty neonová a default a udělej cca 5 dalších. Používej gradienty, ale jen z tmavé do ještě tmavší. Světlé gradienty nejsou hezké"): Půlnoc, Les, Bordó, Uhel, Lilek. **A gradient only runs from a dark step down to a slightly darker step of the same hue** (0.03 L, like Warm paper's footer; 0.09 L was too aggressive, the owner the same day), top to bottom, on the dark header, the dark tile and the footer; light areas (Les's amber tile, pages, cards) and all buttons stay flat. They differ in structure, not only in hue: a dark band with a bright fill (Půlnoc, Les: the tile dark in one, bright in the other), one hue with no light fill (Bordó), a deep grey page (Uhel), and light chrome with the dark in the content (Lilek). The bright accents (cyan, amber, mandarin) are the same recorded exception as the neon: no 3:1 edge on white, their dark label carries them; links are a darker step. 48 pairs per mode computed (text 4.5:1 incl. header text at both ends of each gradient, the tile text at both ends and on hover, footer text, edges and this year's bar 3:1); all pass apart from that exception.

**Earlier rounds, removed** (2026-10-21 and 22; see [decisions.md](decisions.md)): two- and three-colour schemes, one colour with gradients, glowing orange with blue, white page with white or tinted boxes, a colour-field page, a bright pastel palette, contrast pairs, and Neon in other hues. The owner's verdict that led here: schemes built on one formula (a brand-hue header, an action hue, charts in shades) look alike whatever the hue ("liší se vlastně jen základní barvou"); variants must differ in structure, the shares of colour and the greys, not only the hue.

The list of schemes lives in `SCHEMES` (`src/domain/schema.ts`). A saved scheme that is no longer in the list (removed or renamed) falls back to Warm paper (`.catch('warm')`), so removing a scheme can't stop a folder from loading (2026-10-20: removing Pouť, Kraslice and Papoušek did, for someone who had picked one); `src/schemes/schemes.test.ts` checks that each one has its file, is imported, has a name in both languages and redefines every slot in light and dark. Hooks for motifs (`--scheme-header-image`, `--scheme-tile-image`, `--scheme-footer-image` and their `-repeat`, `-position`, `-size`, `--scheme-header-pad`) stay in `src/index.css`; the dark-gradient round uses the header, tile and footer images, and Křída and Lilek draw their header line with `--scheme-header-image`.

## Charts (`ui/BarChart.tsx`, `features/home/RevenueCharts.tsx`)

- Hand-built SVG, with no chart library.
- They follow the dataviz skill's rules:
  - columns at most 24 px wide, a 2 px rounded top, square at the baseline, 2 px gaps
  - light 1 px gridlines
  - a legend only when there are 2 or more series
  - selective value labels (the best year and the current year)
  - a hover tooltip for each band, with the whole band as the hover target
  - a "Tabulka" (Table) view
- **Colors:** focus style. This year is `mid` grey and last year a lighter grey (about 2:1). Lightness separates them, so it's colourblind-safe. The light bars are deliberately faint (the owner's choice), and the Table view has exact values. Legend text is `fg`.
- **Tax donut** (`features/home/TaxEstimate.tsx`, the owner's choice 2026-10-18): centred in the right half of the tax card (the text and rows take the left half; stacked on narrow screens), 280 px, a 34 px ring. Tax, social and health are arcs in `cat-1` … `cat-3`, which each scheme defines from its own hues, with 2 px gaps; what's left of the income is the bare `surface-3` track, with "Zbyde z příjmů", the amount (24 px, 600) and the share in the middle (or "Odvody převyšují příjmy"). Scaled to the income, or to the payments when they are larger. The rows beside it are the legend (a small square of each colour) and the exact values, which is also the relief for light aqua at 2.8:1. Each arc has a `<title>` tooltip. The three colours are a recorded exception to the one-accent rule (see [decisions.md](decisions.md)).
- **Incomplete periods** (the current month or year) use a 45° striped texture, and the tooltip says "zatím, k {date}" (so far, to {date}). Future months have no bar rather than a zero.
- Only revenue (issued plus paid) in CZK is counted. See [exchange-rates.md](exchange-rates.md) for foreign currencies.
