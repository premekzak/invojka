# UI

## Principles (from the owner)

- **Always show the next step.** The Home screen's table of things to check (under the greeting, next to the "Nová faktura" tile; captioned "Co dál" cards until 2026-10-17) comes from the data: create an invoice, N overdue invoices, missing profile fields, review customers. New screens should do the same.
- **Smooth and fast:**
  - keyboard-first editors: the customer search works with ↑ ↓ Enter; Enter in a line description adds the next line, which takes focus
  - no page reloads
  - short rise animations (`animate-rise`)
  - hover actions on list rows
- **Never lose work:** autosave with a visible status; Finish and Issue wait for the save to finish; leaving the editor through "Upravit odběratele" (Edit customer) saves a draft first.
- **Explain in plain words.** The owner isn't a developer. Error and confirmation text says what happened and what to do.

## Design

Invojka is built on the design kit in `src/design/`, documented in **[design/](design/README.md)**: colour roles and state rules ([tokens](design/tokens.md)), the scheme contract and the schemes ([schemes](design/schemes.md)), the type scale ([typography](design/typography.md)), and layout and component patterns ([components](design/components.md)). Those pages are generic; keep them free of invoices and Czech. This section records only what Invojka chooses on top of them. The components are in `src/ui/primitives.tsx` (Button, Input, Select, Textarea, InputAffix, Labeled, Field, Checkbox, ErrorSummary, Card, Choice, SettingRow) and `src/ui/format.tsx` (StatusBadge).

### Faces

- **Source Sans 3 for text, Fira Sans Condensed for headlines** (the owner, 2026-11-01, after trying pairs on the whole app in a dev panel; one family, Inter, from 2026-10-28, Roboto headlines before that). Source Sans 3 400, 500, 600 and 700 (`font-sans`, `font-nav`): body, labels, numbers, the navigation and the wordmark. Fira Sans Condensed 600 (`font-display`): page and section titles, no tracking (a condensed face needs none), never 700. Clearly different faces with a similar x-height, as the pairing rule asks. Both are woff2 subsets (latin + latin-ext covers Czech), embedded by `loadUiFonts` in `src/pdf/fonts.ts`. Source Sans 3 is subset from Adobe's hinted TTFs by `scripts/subset-ui-font.sh` into `src/assets/fonts/` (the `@fontsource` files are unhinted and looked soft on Windows, 2026-11-02); Fira Sans Condensed comes hinted from `@fontsource`.
- **The PDF keeps Inter** (400 and 600 full TTFs, `fonts` in the same file); changing the invoice's face is a separate decision.
- No stylistic sets: Inter's `ss01`, `cv05` and `cv08` went with it. Source Sans 3 tells I, l and 1 apart on its own (l has a tail).
- **The wordmark is lowercase, "invojka"** (the owner, 2026-10-19; `WORDMARK` in `src/ui/Logo.tsx`), in the display face at 18 px, in the header, the welcome screen and the footer. Running text, the window title and the PDF keep "Invojka".

### Terakota, the default scheme

Since 2026-11-20 (the owner: "chtěl bych default schéma terakota a pojďme ho upravit na více moderní barvy, dej mi varianty", then of five trial variants: "terakota a meruňka fajn, ať je nový default, časem se k ní vrátíme"). Its values are in `src/design/schemes/nv-terakota.css` (id `nv-terakota`, `DEFAULT_SCHEME` in `src/domain/schema.ts`); it applies when `<html>` has no `data-scheme` too, so the first paint and the welcome screen use it. A folder that saved another scheme (Jasný oblak included) keeps it. The owner means to come back to it, so expect more tuning. Recorded so nobody "fixes" them:
- **A light accent with a dark label:** buttons are a light vivid apricot `#ffa752` with dark brown text; hover goes more saturated, not darker. A light button has no 3:1 edge on white (the kit's recorded exception), so links and focus take a deeper rust `#ac4403`, and so does the main chart series.
- **The logo is a dark brown square with an apricot letter,** not the action colour.
- **A white page with warm greys** (OKLCH hue 65, very low chroma) instead of the cream of 2026-11-04; a warm grey bar a step off the page with a faint 135° gradient, no rule under it, the current page a white tab; a light warm grey footer.
- **The "Nová faktura" tile** is a pale apricot 135° gradient with brown text. Charts: rust bars fading to apricot, last year's a pale apricot fading to pale yellow; no olive any more.
- **Warnings, arrears and errors are red** (2026-11-05), **and the status colours belong to the scheme** (the owner, 2026-11-27: "Zelená a červená pro status by měly zapadat do schématu"): the red is a brick red (OKLCH hue 31, `#aa3525` on a pale `#ffece6` chip; a cooler crimson `#b3161f` before) and "Zaplaceno" an olive green (hue 136, `#487033` on `#e9f6db`; a cool `#007d3e` before), both lower in chroma. The brick stays about 15° of hue and a clear step of lightness away from the rust of links, and a status always has its word and dot.
- **Chart bars are almost flat** (the owner, the same day: "Gradienty na grafech jsou moc výrazné"): the top of a bar is only about 0.04 L lighter in the bar's own hue (`#e16c10` to `#ee7c32`, `#fac787` to `#ffd29c`); it no longer fades from rust into apricot or from apricot into yellow.
- The other four trial variants (a vivid orange in shades, petrol, sand, plum) were removed; they are described in `schemes/terakota/zadani.md` and [decisions.md](decisions.md).

Its values are in `src/design/schemes/warm.css` and its summary in [design/schemes.md](design/schemes.md#the-schemes). The owner's choices and exceptions, recorded so nobody "fixes" them:
- **A tinted header and a cream page.** The peach band is a large tinted area, and the page is faintly cream rather than white (the colour rules would keep both muted). Picked as "F4" and "O1" on 2026-10-17.
- **Burnt-orange accent** `#cf4a09` with white text (2026-10-18, replacing the blue). It is 3.95:1 on the page, so text links and focus use the darker `link` `#a94e00`.
- **The owner's orange `#fa8d4e`** is only the logo's marks (2.3:1 on white, never text or anything clickable).
- **Ochre for warnings, crimson for errors** (2026-10-17). The owner finds red aggressive, so overdue and missing rates are ochre, always with a word; errors and delete stay crimson because they are rare.
- **Charts in one orange and its shades** (the owner: "at most two colours in charts, then only shades"): `series-1` orange, `series-2` pale peach, the tax donut dark, bar and light orange.
- **Flat everywhere except the footer** (espresso, a slight gradient, the same in both modes). Gradients on buttons, the header and the tile were tried on 2026-10-17 and found too much.

### Schemes in the app

Settings → Aplikace → Barevné schéma (`settings.ui.scheme`); light and dark come from Vzhled, so every scheme has both. `src/App.tsx` sets `<html data-scheme>` (none for the default, `DEFAULT_SCHEME`). The list is `SCHEMES` in `src/domain/schema.ts`; a saved id that is no longer in it falls back to the default (`.catch(DEFAULT_SCHEME)`), so removing a scheme can't stop a folder from loading. `src/domain/schemes.test.ts` checks that every id has a file in the kit, an import in `src/index.css` and a name in both languages. The owner's briefs and reference images are in `schemes/<name>/` (only `zadani.md` is committed).

**On trial since 2026-11-26:** ten mildly green schemes, most with one companion hue, `ze-salvej`, `ze-mata`, `ze-eukalyptus`, `ze-mech` and `ze-jedle`, then a second round where green takes more of the screen (the owner: "zkus ještě nějaké další, více pracuj se zelenou"), `ze-louka`, `ze-hvozd`, `ze-pistacie`, `ze-limetka` and `ze-oliva` (the owner on the first round: "schéma mírně do zelena s jednou doplňkovou barvou, udělej pár variant"; brief in `schemes/zelena/zadani.md`, values in the table in [design/schemes.md](design/schemes.md#the-schemes)). They wait for the owner's pick; the ones not picked are to be removed.

### Where Invojka uses the kit's patterns

- **Header:** a 44 px band the colour of the page (white in Warm paper; the owner, 2026-10-31, lifting the "never white" rule of 2026-10-29): logo and wordmark at the size of the links (a 16 px logo, the wordmark in the text face `font-sans` at 700 and 14 px, never the heading face; links to Home), the five pages as plain text links right after it, all on the left, the business switcher on the right with the save tick before it. The links have no tabs and no icons, 24 px apart, Inter 500 at 14 px, each 32 px tall with 12 px at the sides and 4 px between them; the inactive ones in `header-muted`, the current one in `header-fg` at 600 on a `track` rectangle with `rounded-md` corners (the owner, 2026-10-31, after an underline, a pill and a fainter `surface-2` fill). No sidebar (2026-10-17). "Nová faktura" is not in the header.
- **Save tick:** a `Check` in `header-fg`; its tooltip says "Vše uloženo v 18:05", the folder name and that the browser hides the full path. Saving is a small spinner in `header-muted`; an error or a conflict is a solid crimson chip with words. Demo mode looks the same.
- **Business switcher:** the business name with a `ChevronsUpDown` icon opens a menu aligned right: each business with its folder name under it (only when it differs), a `Check` in `accent` on the current one, a `rule` divider, then "Přidat další firmu" in `accent` with a hint.
- **Footer:** the logo (`.on-inverse`), the wordmark and the tagline on the left; "Bez cloudu a bez účtu. Funguje i offline." and a link to invojka.cz on the right.
- **Feature block:** "Nová faktura" on Home (the owner's choice "K2", 2026-10-17), a 17.5 rem square that stays square however long the list beside it grows (the owner, 2026-11-01; a full-width block on phones), a 44 px plus and a one-line hint. Its colour is light and quiet in every scheme (the owner, 2026-11-01: neither loud nor black): pale apricot with espresso text in Warm paper, pale blue with blue text or a paper shade with ink text in the others. Next to it, at least as tall, a white card listing what needs checking: its first line says how many (ochre alert icon), then one striped row per problem with a dot, a title, a detail and a small link button; overdue invoices are ochre, the rest neutral. With nothing to check the card shows a large check in a green circle in its middle, "Vše v pořádku" and one line of detail (2026-11-01; a single line before).
- **Home greeting** always ends with a full stop, or a question mark for a question (`src/i18n/greetings.test.ts`). **It never addresses a man or a woman** (the owner, 2026-11-27: "oslovení bychom měli upravit pro případ, že je uživatelem žena", then "vymysli jiná oslovení, která nejsou závislá na pohlaví"): where a greeting addresses the user, it uses a mock title that fits anyone ("Vaše Excelence", "Vaše Blahorodí", "Vaše Jasnosti", "hlavo podniku", "legendo fakturace"; "pane Továrníku" before) or greets the firm as an institution ("správní rado", "generální ředitelství", "dozorčí rado", like the older "vážené vedení"); each time of day but the night has one of each. There is no setting for it and the app never asks; the same test checks that no greeting says pan, paní, Mr or Ms. Elsewhere the app speaks to the user in the plural ("překročili jste"), which has no gender either; keep new texts that way. Picked by time of day from `home.greetings.<slot>` (`dawn` 5–9, `morning` 9–12, `noon` 12–14, `afternoon` 14–18, `evening` 18–22, `night`), one per day and slot (`pickDaily`). Never the owner's name: Czech would need the vocative ("Dobrý den, Jana" was wrong). The jokes ("pane Továrníku") were the owner's idea.
- **Home summary:** one or two sentences in the lead size, `max-w-prose`, numbers semibold in `fg`, the overdue part in `warning`, amounts in whole units (`f.moneyWhole`). It covers invoices and revenue this year, the change against the same point last year (`yearTrend`), what's waiting and how much is overdue; its variants: no invoices yet, everything paid, nothing overdue, some overdue, all overdue.
- **VAT registration limit** card (non-payers and identified persons): this year's turnover against 2,000,000 Kč with the percentage, a thin bar (`accent`, `warning` from 80 %), and one muted line on the two limits, the foreign invoices left out and that it's an estimate. From 80 % a task row appears too. Rules in [invoicing-rules.md](invoicing-rules.md#vat-registration-limit).
- **Tax donut** (`features/home/TaxEstimate.tsx`, the owner's choice 2026-10-18): in the right half of the tax card, 280 px with a 34 px ring; tax, social and health in `cat-1` … `cat-3`, the rest of the income as the track with "Zbyde z příjmů", the amount and the share in the middle (or "Odvody převyšují příjmy"). Scaled to the income, or to the payments when they are larger.
- **Revenue charts** (`ui/BarChart.tsx`, `features/home/RevenueCharts.tsx`): only revenue (issued plus paid) in CZK; foreign currencies in [exchange-rates.md](exchange-rates.md). Incomplete periods say "zatím, k {date}".
- **Invoice list:** status labels right-aligned, so they end at the same edge as the amount column (the owner, 2026-10-17); "Zaplaceno" as an icon button before the status. On the customer list "+ Nová faktura" is an `accent` link after the last-invoice date, on hover or focus.
- **Phones:** a closable notice (`ui/DesktopNotice.tsx`, once per visit) says Invojka is a desktop app and why (mobile browsers can't open a folder); Home's revenue box and recent invoices stack. Checked at 390 px on every screen.
- **PDF:** neutral by default (warm ink greys, a near-black "to pay" box; the stamps are ink too). A user may pick one accent in Settings → Vzhled faktury (blue, teal, green, wine, orange; each at least 4.5:1 with white text); it colours only the title and the "to pay" box, so the PDF still prints well in black and white. All PDF colours are `PDF_COLORS` in `src/pdf/palette.ts`, which Settings reads for its swatches. The PDF doesn't follow the app's scheme.
